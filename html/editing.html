What Is Multimedia Creation & Editing?

Multimedia creation is the process of producing content using different forms of digital media such as images, illustrations, video, audio, animation and screen recordings.

Editing is the process of modifying, arranging, improving and combining these media elements to communicate an idea, story or message.

Simple idea: Multimedia is about combining different types of digital content to create something people can see, hear and experience.

Core Concepts You Should Understand

Resolution Number of pixels in an image or video
Frame Rate Number of video frames displayed per second
Bitrate Amount of data used per unit of time
Layers Separate editable visual elements
Timeline Sequence used for arranging media
Keyframes Points that define changes over time
Codecs Methods used to encode and decode media
Color Correction Adjusting color and exposure
Audio Waveform Visual representation of an audio signal
Compression Reducing media file size

Things People Often Don't Know

01

Image, video and audio editing are closely connected. A single project can require several different tools.

02

Good editing is not only about adding effects. Removing unnecessary elements is often more important.

03

Audio quality can strongly affect how professional a video feels, even when the visuals are good.

04

Open-source editing software can be powerful enough for serious creative projects.
