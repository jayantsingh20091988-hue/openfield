Local AI

Run powerful AI models directly on your own computer, with more control, privacy and independence from cloud services.

What Is Local AI?

Local AI means running artificial intelligence models directly on your own computer instead of sending every request to a remote cloud server.

This allows developers, researchers and curious users to experiment with AI while having greater control over their data, models and computing environment.

Why Does Local AI Matter?

🔒

Privacy

Data can remain on your own machine instead of being sent to an external AI service.

⚡

Local Processing

Once a model is available locally, you can interact with it without depending entirely on a remote API.

🧪

Experimentation

Developers can test different models, prompts and AI workflows on their own systems.

🧠

Understanding AI

Local AI provides a practical way to learn about models, inference and AI applications.

Core Concepts You Should Understand

Large Language Models

AI models trained to understand and generate human-like text.

Inference

The process of using a trained model to produce an output from an input.

Parameters

Values learned by a model during training that influence its behaviour.

Tokens

Pieces of text that AI models process.

Quantization

Reducing numerical precision to make models smaller and easier to run.

Context Window

The amount of information a model can consider during an interaction.

How Does Ollama Work?

The basic idea is simple: 


Choose a model
      ↓
Download the model
      ↓
Ollama loads the model
      ↓
Your prompt is processed locally
      ↓
The model generates a response
      ↓
Response appears on your computer

The exact performance depends on the model, available RAM, CPU, GPU and other hardware characteristics.

Hardware Requirements

CPU

A modern multi-core CPU can run smaller models, although performance varies significantly.

RAM

More RAM allows you to work with larger models.

GPU

A compatible GPU with sufficient VRAM can significantly improve local AI performance.

Storage

AI models can require several gigabytes of storage, so free disk space matters.

Advantages

  • Greater control over your data
  • Can work without constant cloud communication
  • Useful for experimentation
  • Open-source ecosystem
  • Can integrate with your own applications
  • Useful for learning AI development

Limitations

  • Large models can require powerful hardware
  • Local models may be slower than cloud services on weaker computers
  • Model quality varies between models
  • Storage requirements can become large
  • Setting up advanced AI systems requires technical knowledge

Alternatives

llama.cpp

Lightweight inference framework focused on running language models efficiently.

Open WebUI

A web-based interface that can be used with local AI backends.

GPT4All

Desktop-oriented local AI software for interacting with local language models.

Things People Often Don't Know

01

Local AI does not automatically mean every model will work offline with no setup.

02

Model size is only one factor affecting performance.

03

Quantization can make large models much more practical on consumer hardware.

04

Local AI can be combined with ordinary web applications and programming languages.
