What Is Electronics & PCB Design?

Electronics is the field of designing and working with circuits, components, signals, sensors, microcontrollers, and electronic systems.

PCB design is the process of turning an electronic circuit schematic into a physical circuit board that connects all components together.

Simple idea: You first design how the circuit should work, then design how the physical board should be arranged.

Things People Often Don't Know

01

A PCB is not simply a board with components. Its physical layout directly affects electrical behavior.

02

At high frequencies, even PCB traces can behave like transmission lines.

03

Good PCB design requires both electrical knowledge and physical/manufacturing awareness.

04

KiCad can be used for projects ranging from simple hobby boards to complex professional hardware.
