YouTube has become one of the largest free learning platforms in the world. The right channels can help students understand difficult concepts, explore new subjects and develop practical skills.

This page collects useful educational channels and organizes them by subject so you can quickly find something worth learning.

How Should You Use YouTube for Learning?

01

Learn the concept

Use a good explanation to understand something you find difficult.

02

Practice yourself

Close the video and solve problems, write code or apply the idea yourself.

03

Go deeper

Once the basics are clear, move toward advanced resources and projects.

Quick Summary

The best educational YouTube channel is not necessarily the most popular one. Choose the resource that explains concepts clearly and helps you actually learn and practice.
