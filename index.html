THE IDEA

What is OpenField?

Learning technology shouldn't begin with “Which expensive software should I buy?”

It should begin with “What do I want to understand or create?”

OpenField connects interesting fields of technology, science, engineering and digital creativity with powerful tools that are free or open source.

Instead of giving you a giant list of software, each field explains what the field actually is, why it matters, what you can build with it, which tools are worth learning and where to go next.
